# How customers perceive credit limit increases

## Key finding
Customers read a limit increase as a reward for good behavior, not as an invitation to spend more. Framing it as recognition increases trust.

## Risks
Unrequested increases announced with celebration language made 22% of interviewed customers worried about overspending.

## Recommendations
Use neutral, respectful language, show the new limit next to the option to keep the old one, and never use urgency such as "use it now".
