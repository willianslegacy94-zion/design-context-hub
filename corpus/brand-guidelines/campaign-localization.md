# Campaign localization playbook

## Central templates, local adaptation
Campaigns are produced centrally as templates. Local teams in Mexico and Colombia adapt copy, imagery and offers, but never the logo, the primary color or the layout grid.

## What local teams may change
Headline and body copy, photography (must show local people and places), offer values and legal disclaimers required by the local regulator.

## Review flow
Every localized piece goes through one brand review (48h SLA) and one legal review per country. Pieces that only change copy within an approved template skip brand review.

## Imagery rules
Real customers over stock photos. No luxury cues such as yachts or sports cars: the brand promise is financial empowerment, not status.
