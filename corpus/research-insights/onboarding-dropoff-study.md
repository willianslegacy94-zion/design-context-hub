# Onboarding drop-off study

## Key finding
38% of applicants abandon onboarding at the document photo step. Most retries happen because of glare and blurry photos on low-end Android phones.

## Method
Funnel analysis of 120k sessions plus 14 moderated interviews in São Paulo and Recife.

## Recommendations
Add a live camera guide with automatic capture, explain why the document is needed before opening the camera, and allow saving progress so users can finish later.

## Impact so far
A pilot with automatic capture reduced drop-off at this step from 38% to 27%.
