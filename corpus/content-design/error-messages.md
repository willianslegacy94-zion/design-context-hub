# Writing error messages

## Formula
What happened + why (if useful) + what to do next. Maximum two short sentences.

## Examples
Bad: "Error 4012. Invalid operation." Good: "We could not send your payment because the account number is incomplete. Check the number and try again."

## Never
Blame the customer, show internal codes as the main message, or use exclamation marks in errors.
