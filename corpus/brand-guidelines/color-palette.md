# Color palette

## Primary color
Orbit Violet (#6B2BD9) is the primary brand color. Use it for primary actions, key highlights and the logo. Never use it for error or warning states.

## Neutrals
Ink (#1A1523) for body text, Mist (#F4F1FA) for page backgrounds, Stone (#8A8494) for secondary text. Pure black (#000000) is not part of the palette.

## Contrast rules
Text on Orbit Violet must be white and at least 16px. Every text/background pair must reach WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text).

## Semantic colors
Success Green (#1E9E6A), Warning Amber (#E59A1C), Error Red (#D93B3B). Semantic colors are for states only, never for decoration or campaigns.
