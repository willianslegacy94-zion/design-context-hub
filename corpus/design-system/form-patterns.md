# Form patterns

## Inputs
Labels always visible above the field; placeholders are only examples. One column on mobile.

## Validation
Validate on blur, not on every keystroke. Show the error below the field, in Error Red, with an icon, and announce it to screen readers.

## Sensitive data
Mask document numbers after entry and allow users to reveal them with a toggle.
