# Voice and tone

## Voice
Clear, human and direct. We explain money like a knowledgeable friend: no jargon, no fine print, no blame.

## Tone by moment
Celebratory only for achievements the customer chose, such as paying off a loan. Calm and practical for problems. Never playful when money is lost or blocked.

## Words we avoid
"Unfortunately", "simply", "just", and any word that suggests the customer did something wrong.
