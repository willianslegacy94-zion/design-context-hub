# Color palette (2024)

## Primary color
The primary brand color is Royal Purple (#820AD1). Use it for buttons and highlights.

## Neutrals
Use pure black (#000000) for body text and white for backgrounds.

## Contrast rules
Aim for good contrast whenever possible.
