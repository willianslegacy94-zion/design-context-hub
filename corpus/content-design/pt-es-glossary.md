# Portuguese and Spanish product glossary

## Core terms
Credit limit: "limite" (BR), "límite de crédito" (MX, CO). Bill: "fatura" (BR), "estado de cuenta" (MX), "extracto" (CO). Instant transfer: "Pix" (BR), "SPEI" (MX), "Bre-B" (CO).

## Rules
Never translate brand names or product names. Use "você" in Brazil and "tú" in Mexico and Colombia.
