# Button component

## Variants
Primary (Orbit Violet fill), Secondary (violet outline), Ghost (text only) and Destructive (Error Red fill). One primary button per screen.

## Shape and size
Corner radius 12px for all variants. Minimum height 48px and minimum touch target 48x48px.

## Accessibility
Icon-only buttons require an accessible label. Disabled buttons must explain why they are disabled nearby.

## Copy
Use verbs that describe the outcome ("Pay bill", "Send money"), not generic labels ("OK", "Continue").
