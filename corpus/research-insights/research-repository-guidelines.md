# How to tag research in the repository

## Required tags
Every study needs: product area, method (interview, survey, usability test, analytics), country and date. Studies without these tags are not searchable by other teams.

## Evidence links
Link raw notes and recordings, but never paste personal data (names, document numbers, phone numbers) into the summary.

## Retention
Recordings are deleted after 12 months; summaries are kept indefinitely.
