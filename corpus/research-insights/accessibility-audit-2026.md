# Accessibility audit 2026

## Scope
42 core screens of the mobile app tested with TalkBack, VoiceOver and font scaling at 200%.

## Top issues
Icon-only buttons without labels (31 occurrences), error messages not announced by screen readers, and amounts read digit by digit instead of as currency.

## Fixes required
Every icon button needs an accessible label. Errors must be announced and linked to their field. Money values need a screen-reader label such as "1,250 reais".
